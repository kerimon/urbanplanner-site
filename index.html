How to order

Ordering & Payment

This is the usual process with construction and equipment-hire companies. Terms below are our suggested defaults.

Renting equipment

1️⃣

Choose & add to cart

Pick machines, then set the number of units and days.

2️⃣

Place your order

Enter your details, site address and payment method at checkout.

3️⃣

We confirm

We check availability, assess the site and confirm delivery cost.

4️⃣

Pay in Naira

A 50% deposit confirms the booking; the balance is due before delivery.

5️⃣

Delivery & return

Machine and operator arrive on site and are collected when the hire ends.

Construction projects

📝

Request a quote

Describe your project using the form in the Contact section.

📐

Site visit & costing

We inspect the site and prepare a bill of quantities.

📄

Proposal & contract

You review the price, timeline and sign the contract.

💳

Deposit & milestones

Pay a mobilisation deposit, then stage payments as work progresses.

Payment methods (all in ₦ Naira)

🏦

Bank transfer

Transfer to our account and send the receipt with your order reference.

💳

Card / online link

We send a secure Naira payment link for debit card or bank payment.

💵

Cash on site

Pay the agreed amount to our representative on delivery.
